How to

  1. On the PC: python server.py in the vrdesktop folder.
  2. Open this page as http://localhost:8000/host.html and press Share screen (pick Entire screen; tick "Also share system audio" for sound).
  3. On the phone (same Wi-Fi): open the https address shown above. The first time it warns that the connection isn't private: tap Advanced, then Proceed. Type the code, press Enter VR.
  4. Mouse control (optional): also run python control.py and tick the box above.

The phone and this PC must be on the same Wi-Fi; nothing goes through the internet.
