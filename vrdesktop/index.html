Drag / arrows: look · Click: tap · Wheel: closer / further · +/-: size · F: curved · M: mouse · Esc: menu

VR Desktop

Your PC's screen on a giant virtual monitor, for a phone in a Cardboard headset.

How to use
  • On the PC open http://localhost:8000/host.html, press Share screen and type its code here
  • Enter VR and put the phone sideways into the headset. Look at the screen: that's where it stays
  • Look down for the buttons: recenter, size, distance, curved / flat, mouse, sound. Look at one and tap
  • Double tap = recenter (with Mouse ON: double tap while looking away from the screen)
  • Mouse ON (if the PC allows it): look + tap = click, tap twice = double click, hold still = right click, hold and tilt your head up / down = scroll
  • Or just use the PC's own mouse and keyboard: the pointer shows on the virtual screen
  • Leave VR with the phone's back gesture
