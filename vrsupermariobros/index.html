All controls

Phone VR headset (Enter VR: the phone goes fullscreen side by side; turn it sideways into the headset)

  • Look around / steer: turn your head and body
  • Walk: walk in place. Run: jog in place
  • Jump: hop, or tap the screen / headset button
  • No room to move? Hold the screen / button to walk forward
  • To leave VR: use the phone's back gesture / button
  • Warp pipe: stand on it and look down into it
  • Fire Mario: look at an enemy to throw fireballs

VR controllers

  • Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn
  • A / X or tap trigger: jump. Hold trigger: walk forward
  • B / Y or grip: run and fire. Stick click: crouch

Keyboard and mouse

  • WASD / arrows: move and turn. Mouse: look (click to capture)
  • SPACE: jump. SHIFT: run. F / click: fireball. C: enter pipe
  • P: pause. M: mute. R: recenter VR view
