Before you ride

Phone in a Cardboard headset: press Enter VR, turn the phone sideways into the headset and sit down (on a chair that can't spin away, this one is intense). Look forward when the ride starts: that becomes straight ahead.

Look at balloons to pop them. Gold stars are worth 5 and hide in tricky places: look up, sideways, behind you. Tap the screen (headset button) to throw your hands up and scream.

How to ride
  • The ride starts by itself after a few seconds (or tap)
  • Look around by turning your head; double tap to recenter
  • Look at a balloon to pop it; the reticle turns yellow when you're on target
  • Tap / hold = hands up. At the end: tap to ride again
  • Leave VR with the phone's back gesture
  • On a screen: drag or arrow keys to look, space = hands up, C = chase camera, M = mute

Not for anyone who gets motion sick easily. Take the headset off if you feel unwell.

Everything (track, models, sounds and music) is generated in code.
