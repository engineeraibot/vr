🔨 Whack-a-Mole 🐹VR PASSTHROUGH

Moles pop out of little floating islands in your own room. Swat them with your real hands before they duck back down, and don't whack the bombs! 3 lives, faster every 10 moles, a life back every 5 levels.

Hands and camera

In the headset the phone's back camera shows you the room and watches your hands. Keep your hands up in front of you where the camera can see them; a yellow ring shows each hand it's tracking.

🏆 Leaderboard

How to play

Phone in a VR headset (the back camera must not be covered)

  • Stand or sit with some space around you. Enter VR and put the phone sideways into the headset
  • Swat the mole holding the START sign to begin. Turn your head to see all nine holes
  • 🐹 Mole = +1 point. 💣 Bomb = −1 life
  • Double tap the screen (headset button) to recenter the holes in front of you
  • No camera? Look at a mole and tap to whack it
  • If the room seems to swing when you turn your head, change Camera field of view: smaller if the room moves against your head, bigger if it follows your head
  • Leave VR with the phone's back gesture

On a screen

  • Webcam: stand back so the camera sees your hands and swat the moles on screen
  • Or click / tap the moles, or use the keys Q W E / A S D / Z X C (one per hole)
  • M: mute. Esc: back to this menu

Based on the eye-toy Whack-a-Mole. All models, sounds and music are generated in code.
