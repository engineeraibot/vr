SUPER KART64 VR

CASTLE RACEWAY · 3 LAPS · 8 RACERS

Hand steering

Hold both hands up in front of the camera like on a steering wheel. Left hand higher turns right, right hand higher turns left. No hands: the kart stops.

How to play

Phone in a VR headset (the back camera must not be covered)

  • Sit down. Enter VR, turn the phone sideways into the headset
  • Both hands up = drive. Tilt them like a wheel to steer. Hands down = stop
  • Look around by turning your head
  • Orange arrow pads give a turbo; you need speed for the big jump over the lake
  • No camera? Tilt your head to steer and hold the screen to drive
  • Leave VR with the phone's back gesture

On a screen

  • Webcam hands, or arrow keys / WASD (up = gas), or a gamepad
  • Phone: hold the left / right half of the screen to steer and drive
  • C: first person / chase camera. M: mute
